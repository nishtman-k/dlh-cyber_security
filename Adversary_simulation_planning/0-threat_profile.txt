# Threat intelligence research - FIN7

Threat Actor Profile: FIN7 (G0046)

Target Sectors:
- Financial services
- Retail
- Restaurants and hospitality

Motivation:
FIN7 is a financially motivated cybercrime group. Its main objective
is to generate profit by stealing payment card data and other valuable
information, as well as conducting ransomware operations.

Key Techniques:
1. T1566.001: Phishing: Spearphishing Attachment (Initial Access)
2. T1218.005: System Binary Proxy Execution: Mshta (Defense Evasion)
3. T1059.001: Command and Scripting Interpreter: PowerShell (Execution)

Associated Tools:
- Carbanak
- Cobalt Strike
- POWER SOURCE (POWERSOURCE)

Known Campaign:
U.S. Payment Card Theft Campaign (2015-2018):
FIN7 targeted more than 100 U.S. businesses, primarily restaurants,
gaming companies, and hospitality organizations, using phishing
emails and malware. By August 2018, authorities reported the theft
of over 15 million payment card records from more than 3,600 business
locations.
