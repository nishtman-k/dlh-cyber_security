## 6. Safety, abort and emergency protocols

The safety_plan.md protocol governs. At a missed deadline T, all testing pauses; handler calls Alex, then Samira and sends SMS. Retry backup channel at T+2; call Claire, Marc if necessary, and the active site responder at T+5. Inform provider alternate Léa at T+10. Without verified safe contact with both testers by T+15, abort the whole engagement and request appropriate welfare assistance. Immediate danger is escalated immediately, not after the timeout.

Duress phrase: **“Please bring the blue folder.”** Handler replies “Understood,” triggers the immediate-danger protocol, informs client/site response and aborts both sites. For a real medical/fire emergency call 112; real police emergency 113. Exercise role-play uses simulated dispatch, never real emergency numbers.

If challenged: stop, remain calm, show genuine identification and an actually executed authorization in any real engagement, and cooperate. No claim of immunity. No untrained rescue or running from authorities. After abort, resumption requires both client owner and provider lead's documented approval and a fresh safety briefing.
