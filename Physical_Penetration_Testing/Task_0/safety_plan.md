## 2. Buddy and check-in protocol

Stay together with direct awareness of one another. No independent side missions. If separated, stop testing, inform the handler and regroup at the designated safe point; do not enter excluded or hazardous space to look for a teammate. Let trained responders handle unsafe searches. A lost buddy or missed check-in is not a reason to break scope.

Both sessions run 09:00–17:00 CET with confirmed check-ins at **09:00, 11:00, 13:00, 15:00 and 17:00**. Check in additionally on departure, arrival, challenge and incident. Each check-in gives both names, exact training-map position, welfare, last action and next deadline. Handler acknowledgment is mandatory; sending an unanswered message is insufficient. Log all times and any explicitly agreed revised deadline; never exceed a two-hour interval.

If the handler cannot continue, stop until Léa accepts a logged handover. If communications cannot be verified at start, do not begin. If they fail during testing, cease assessment and use the approved safe route to restore contact.

## 3. Missed check-in and engagement abort

T is the missed scheduled deadline. Testing is paused immediately at T. The handler logs every call/action and uses the following order:

| Deadline | Required action |
| --- | --- |
| T | Call Alex (SIM-2001), then Samira (SIM-2002); send SMS to both. Mark team contact lost and pause all sessions. |
| T+2 minutes | Retry both by voice and private exercise chat. Review last-known position, travel plan and known hazards. |
| T+5 minutes | Call Claire (SIM-1001), then Marc (SIM-1002) if unanswered; then call Paul (SIM-4001) at HQ or Inès (SIM-4002) at SAT. Ask for safe welfare verification within their authority. |
| T+10 minutes | Notify Léa (SIM-3002), the non-deployed provider alternate; prepare the two names, last-known position, times and hazards for responders. Continue contact attempts. |
| T+15 minutes | If both testers' safety is not verified, **abort the entire NFG-PPT-001 engagement at both sites**. Notify all reachable team/client contacts. For a real non-immediate welfare concern use the relevant police non-emergency line; suspected danger goes immediately to emergency services. |

Example: an unanswered 11:00 check-in triggers retries at 11:02, client/site escalation at 11:05, provider-alternate escalation at 11:10 and engagement abort at 11:15. These are maximum deadlines, not waiting periods when harm is suspected.

If contact returns before T+15, remain paused until both testers are verified safe and Claire plus Alex approve a logged resume. After abort, require documented client/provider restart approval, a fresh safety briefing and a new session release. Nobody self-authorizes a restart.

## 4. Duress, medical emergency and confrontation

Duress phrase: **“Please bring the blue folder.”** Card reminder: **BLUE FOLDER**. It signals danger without stating that to a bystander. Handler replies “Understood,” does not seek an explanation in front of the third party, immediately initiates the appropriate emergency response, then notifies Claire and the active site's responder. Abort both sites. Contact the buddy only if safe. Do not wait for a scheduled check-in or T+15 and do not improvise a rescue.

For a real injury/fire call 112; for an immediate police threat call 113. Provide facts, actual location and callback details and follow dispatch instructions. First aid is limited to training; do not move an injured person except to avoid immediate danger. During role-play, use simulated dispatch SIM-9000, never real emergency lines.

If challenged by staff, guards or police: stop, keep hands visible, remain calm and cooperate. Use genuine ID and an actually executed letter in a real engagement. Request client verification; do not flee, resist, destroy evidence or claim immunity. Client confirmation does not overrule lawful authority.
