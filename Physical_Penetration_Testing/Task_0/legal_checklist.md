| Trespass / unlawful entry | Luxembourg **Code pénal, Art. 439** concerns specified entries into another's inhabited premises/dependencies. It is not a generic rule covering every office entrance. | Lock picks / false keys | **Code pénal, Arts. 487–488** address false keys and fraudulent counterfeiting/alteration, including electronic keys. These provisions do **not establish a blanket ban on mere possession** of every lock pick. The review does not establish that every carrying/import/use situation is lawful. [Official code](https://legilux.public.lu/eli/etat/leg/code/penal/20250311/fr) | [x] No lock picks, bumping, shimming or cloning in base scope. Separate legal/tool-use review before any amendment; do not treat professional intent as immunity. |
| Computer/access-system misuse | **Code pénal, Arts. 509-1–509-3** address fraudulent system access and interference/data offences. [Official code](https://legilux.public.lu/eli/etat/leg/code/penal/20250311/fr) | [x] Supervised test badges only; no controller access, live-system testing or production data manipulation. |
| Recording consent | **Loi du 11 août 1982 concernant la protection de la vie privée, Art. 2(1)–(2)** covers private words and a person's image in a private place. Do not assume a US-style one-party permission rule. The CNPD's official 2004 opinion explains the consent issue for participants as well as third parties. [Act](https://legilux.public.lu/eli/etat/leg/loi/1982/08/11/n6/jo); [CNPD interpretation](https://cnpd.public.lu/content/dam/cnpd/fr/decisions-avis/2004/avis-protection-donnees-communication/doc_avis_pdl5181.pdf) | [x] No covert audio/video. Notes and photographs of controls without identifiable people/private content; any expanded recording needs a lawful basis, notices and applicable consent review first. |
| Landlord consent and lease limits | **Code civil, Arts. 1719 and 1728** concern landlord obligations and tenant use; they do not create a universal stand-alone 'pentest permit'. Consent depends on property rights, the lease and proposed actions. [Official court decision discussing both provisions](https://justice.public.lu/content/dam/justice/fr/jurisprudence/cour-cassation/bail-loyer/2022/10/20221027-cas-2021-00108-125p.pdf) | [x] The assignment expressly requires landlord consent: SIM-LC-001 is assumed granted only for NFG Suite S1 and escorted transit/staging. | Civil liability / damage | **Code civil, Arts. 1382–1383** concern fault/negligence causing damage. ## Source and interpretation notes

- The penal-code consolidation accessible through official indexed content was dated 11 March 2025; the live Legilux text required JavaScript in direct fetches. Do not present it as independently verified complete law as of the future session dates. Recheck the applicable version and any subsequent amendments for operational use.
- The privacy statute is from 1982; the cited CNPD interpretation is from 2004. The reviewed material supports a conservative no-covert-recording plan, not a complete account of every statutory exception or current evidentiary rule.
- The civil-law sources are official judicial decisions referring to statutory articles, not claims that those cases decided the legality of physical pentesting. The proposed landlord-consent requirement comes explicitly from the assignment and scenario lease assumption.
- The insurance consolidation is dated 25 November 2025. Verify current coverage and policy conditions directly with a real insurer for live work; no universal legally required €2m limit is asserted.
- No exemption or broad legal conclusion is made for active RF work, waste searching, destructive methods or employee surveillance: these are excluded rather than authorized through an incomplete legal review. Workplace/fire-safety review is required before altering any door or life-safety system; this exercise permits no such alteration.
