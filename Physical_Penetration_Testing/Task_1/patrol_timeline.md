## Limitations and recommendations

The exercise provides one schedule, not repeated observations demonstrating routine predictability. No exact patrol route, guard count beyond this schedule, backup officer, radio coverage or remote-monitoring capability is given. Recommended follow-up is continuity of lobby coverage during breaks, clear relief ownership, and verification of monitoring and incident response. Keep patrol records restricted; do not publish operational schedules for real facilities.
